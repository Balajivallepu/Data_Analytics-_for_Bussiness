# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze employee demographics, workforce distribution, and attrition trends using the IBM HR Analytics dataset.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard helps HR managers understand workforce data through interactive visualizations and KPIs. It supports better decision-making for employee retention, workforce planning, and organizational analysis.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Develop an HR Analytics Dashboard that provides insights into:

- Employee demographics
- Workforce distribution
- Department performance
- Employee attrition
- Job roles
- Education fields
- Business travel
- Income analysis

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance

**Source:** Kaggle

The dataset includes employee information such as:

- Age
- Gender
- Department
- Job Role
- Education
- Monthly Income
- Years at Company
- Business Travel
- Marital Status
- Attrition

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

- Total Employees
- Attrition Count
- Active Employees
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Monthly Income

### Charts

- Employees by Department
- Gender Distribution
- Attrition Status
- Employees by Job Role
- Age Group Distribution
- Education Field Distribution
- Income by Job Level
- Department-wise Attrition
- Business Travel Distribution

### Interactive Slicers

- Department
- Gender
- Attrition
- Job Role
- Education Field
- Business Travel
- Marital Status
- Age Group

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Monitor employee attrition across departments.
- Analyze workforce demographics by age and gender.
- Identify departments with higher employee turnover.
- Compare salary trends across job levels.
- Support HR planning using interactive filters.
